# Phase 3: optical excitation and fluorescence measurement

All generated data are SIMULATION. No experimental dataset is fitted or imported.
Published fitted rate values belong to the original authors' defects. Passing tests
establishes numerical consistency and limited literature benchmarks, not experimental
validation of this project, a sample, a detector, a clock, or its accuracy.

## OPM1. Model lineage and units

The seven-electronic-state cycle has ground and excited spin triplets, each ordered
+1,0,-1, and one effective singlet. TET2012 Sec.2.1, Eqs.(1)-(5), describes this
room-temperature cycle. ROB2011 Secs.3-6 supplies independent lifetime/ISC context,
but its five-state treatment combines +/-1 and is not our seven-state derivation.
PAN2024 supports driven Lindblad and number-resolved counting architecture. Its
accessible arXiv v1 has a different title; equation locators refer to that version.
HIN2018 likewise has explicit accessible-manuscript locators in sources.json.

Internal populations sum to one PER CENTER. All decay/pump/dephasing rates are
s^-1. The existing ground engine returns H/h in cyclic Hz. Only the coherent
Liouvillian multiplies it by 2*pi: -i*2*pi[H/h,rho]. A rate quoted in microseconds^-1
is multiplied by 1e6, never by 2*pi. No runtime dependency is added.

## OPM2. Complete published fitted rate preset

TET2012 published PDF, Table 1, printed p.9, defect NV E:
radiative=63.7 +/-4.5; ISC0=9.3 +/-3.0; ISCpm=53.0 +/-5.9;
singlet0=0.9 +/-0.8; singletpm=0.5 +/-0.2, all microseconds^-1.
Each pm rate is for EACH spin state. Singlet total return is q0+2*qpm.
Uncertainties are recorded but not propagated: no joint covariance is available.
Overrides are classified simulation_assumption_override, not published values.
Derived total excited lifetimes are 13.6986 ns and 8.5690 ns; singlet lifetime is
526.316 ns. TET2012 Fig.3(c), printed p.8, reports 13.7 ns and 8.6 ns at zero field
for this defect. This consistency uses that same fitted set, not independent
experimental validation. Do not use inverse total lifetime as a radiative rate and
then add ISC again. Optical-rate temperature dependence is NOT modeled.

## OPM3. Laser, fluorescence and shelving

Each bare spin channel has g_m -> e_m at W, e_m -> g_m at gamma_r,
e_m -> s at k_m, and s -> g_m at q_m. In the master equation each transition is a
separate sqrt(rate)|target><origin| jump. Emission follows gamma_r times total
excited population. Spin contrast arises from competing ISC, not manually assigned
brightness coefficients. Singlet return to +/-1 is retained. Optical excitation is
incoherent, appropriate to the intended nonresonant green excitation; no optical
carrier oscillations, vibronic dynamics or coherent optical Rabi physics are modeled.
The effective singlet does not predict 1042-nm spectroscopy or separate singlet levels.

W is the configurable effective pump rate. W=c_L*laser_power could be calibrated by
an experiment; no universal power conversion or absorption cross section is asserted.
Default W=2e6/s, Rabi=200 kHz, pure dephasing=1e5/s and symmetric electron ground-pair
relaxation=1000/s are MODEL assumptions, not fits. Population saturation emerges from
the cycle; count rates depend on detection assumptions. Small direct optical
spin-flip channels, charge conversion and nonradiative channels beyond ISC are omitted.

## OPM4. Microwave and rotating-wave conventions

The ground eigenbasis is used. States with m_s=0 overlap >0.99 have rotating-frame
index q=0; states with overlap <0.01 have q=1. Ambiguous assignments are rejected.
H_rot/h=diag(E_i - f_MW*q_i) + (rabi_Hz/2)*sqrt(2)*M_between_manifolds.
The common energy origin is immaterial. M is Sx in seven states and the existing
normalized electronic/nuclear microwave operator in 21 states. Thus an isolated
pure 0<->+1 line has the specified Rabi cycles/s. Matrix elements are retained;
the envelope coupling in angular units is Omega_ij/2, with
Omega_ij=4*pi*abs((H_rot/h)_ij). Counter-rotating terms and Bloch-Siegert shifts are
omitted. Nuclear-only transitions are not addressed by this GHz drive.

Ground pure-dephasing jumps sqrt(gamma_phi)|i><i| for EACH ground eigenstate make
an inter-ground coherence decay at gamma_phi. Additional optical and ground-state
outflow also dephase it; the quoted rate is not an imposed final ODMR linewidth.
Ground relaxation uses symmetric per-electron-pair bare rates, projected by overlaps;
these are a phenomenological high-temperature model, not a measured T1 constant.
No optical excited-spin coherence is generated by the independent channels: their
axial diagonal energies do not affect populations and are omitted. Excited transverse
mixing/hyperfine coupling are not represented. A density matrix alone cannot repair
these omissions outside the stated scope.

## OPM5. Rate backend and numerical solution

For no MW coupling the diagonal sector exactly obeys dot(p)=K p, with K_ji the
transition rate i->j, columns summing to zero. Independent optical jumps are defined
in the chosen eigenbasis; this reduction is not a claim of exact physical nuclear
coherence dynamics. The CW MW approximation eliminates each steady Bloch coherence:
W_ij=Omega_ij^2*Gamma2_ij / [2*(Gamma2_ij^2+Delta_ij^2)],
Delta_ij=2*pi*(H_ii-H_jj)/h. Gamma2_ij includes half the total outflow from each
state plus pure dephasing. Derivation follows steady Bloch Eq.(16) in DRE2011
accessible arXiv appendix. This is approximate for shared-state coherent pathways,
strong driving, transients and dark-state interference. It is not used for Rabi tests.
The default/reference backend is Lindblad. Rate outputs are always labeled 'rate'.
The exported benchmark grid quantifies disagreement rather than certifying universal
equivalence. Sparse steady linear solves enforce trace and check positivity/residuals.
Disconnected sectors use a checked minimum-norm stationary solution; it chooses an
equal spectator mixture in the tested decoupled limit, not an arbitrary initial
nuclear preparation. Uniform-grid transients propagate a supplied initial state.

## OPM6. Twenty-one-state extension and operating boundary

Implementation sequence: seven-state tests passed first (5 tests); only then was
21-state support added. H_g is the existing hamiltonian(297,Bz,0,Physics) WITHOUT
changing any coefficient or matrix element. Its exact eigenvalues/eigenvectors are
used, including transverse hyperfine mixing already present in that Hamiltonian.
Nine ground eigenstates, nine bare excited (m_s,m_I) states and three effective
singlet m_I labels give 21 states. No new nitrogen Hamiltonian replaces H_g.

Extension assumptions: for an optical bare channel a=(m_s,m_I), pump i->e_a and
radiative e_a->i are weighted by |<a|g_i>|^2. ISC e_a->s_mI and singlet return to
g_i use the same nuclear label and bare overlaps. These are INDEPENDENT rank-one
channels. This secular choice destroys coherences between different nuclear/ground
optical channels; it is NOT a coherent dipole tensor identity on the nucleus and
must not be used to validate nuclear Ramsey readout or nuclear coherence lifetime.
Excited/singlet nuclear splittings, hyperfine terms, nuclear coherences transferred
through emission, and dynamic nuclear polarization are omitted. Optical cycles can
redistribute ground eigenstates through small overlaps; resulting nuclear populations
are predictions of this approximation, not calibrated polarization measurements.
This extension is numerically tested, not experimentally validated.

Accepted range: single orientation in its local frame; purely axial positive
Bz=5..50 G; E=0; nominal room temperature fixed to 297 K; W=0..20e6/s;
Rabi=0..1 MHz. This is a conservative PROJECT scope, not a paper-established universal
validity boundary. Field endpoints and selected intermediate settings have numerical
tests. No promise of optical accuracy throughout the box is made. There are no API
transverse/electric-field inputs silently ignored. Existing four-orientation ODMR
and its broader parameter scope remain available unchanged. Physical optical
orientation ensembles with off-axis mixing are future work.

In particular 475 G is rejected. Low-field numerical checks do not establish optical
validity near the excited-state anticrossing around 510 G; excited-state hyperfine
and nuclear-polarization physics need explicit modeling and validation there.
No Phase 3 fluorescence or linewidth is fed into the existing clock comparison.

## OPM7. Synthetic acquisition and contrast

CW scan frequencies cover the upper electronic branch +/- configured span, including
14N hyperfine features in the 21-state model. Each paired off/on gate begins in its
own computed stationary state. Acquisition timestamps concatenate actual gates;
stationary preparation overhead is EXCLUDED. This is an idealized reset protocol,
not a continuous time sweep, feedback loop or initialized finite-duration scan.
A separate laser-on transient starts from a completely mixed ground state.

Expected detected counts per gate = duration * [N_centers*efficiency*gamma_r*
excited_population + optical_background + dark_rate]. Centers are independent and
identical in this single orientation. No collective optical coherence is assumed.
Poisson scan mode is explicitly an approximation, restricted to efficiency <=2% and
gates >=0.1 ms; these guards do not prove Poisson validity for arbitrary rate
overrides. The supplied preset is checked with a number-resolved reference. Do not
interpret the guards as validation of every possible driven setting. Technical
intensity/frequency noise, detector dead time and afterpulsing are omitted.

Intrinsic contrast = 1 - emitted_on/emitted_off. Expected detected contrast includes
background. Sampled contrast=1-counts_on/counts_off; zero denominator yields NaN
with a validity flag; negative values are retained. The largest mean grid contrast
is a truth-selected illustration, not a fitted resonance or unbiased inferred contrast.
No assumed contrast or phenomenological Lorentzian enters this measurement model.
Each row includes every state population, aggregate optical populations, MW frequency,
mean/counts, timing, root seed, stream and stream seed. NumPy PCG64 and SeedSequence
are explicit. Reproduction requires recorded versions as well as seeds.

## OPM8. Number-resolved counting reference

Let J=sum_radiative efficiency*(L* rho L^dagger) be the detected jump superoperator.
Photon-number sectors obey dot(rho_n)=(Lindblad-J)rho_n+J rho_(n-1), beginning with
rho_0=rho_initial. Tr(rho_n) gives P(n). All other loss/emission events remain in
the no-detected-event part; independent losses are therefore correctly retained.
The cutoff uses a bound from the maximal radiative hazard and rejects missing
probability mass above 1e-8. This is a gate count distribution, not an event-arrival
stream. With no MW and diagonal state the invariant diagonal sector is used exactly.
Samples are drawn from P(n); optional independent background is added as Poisson.
Reference export: 4096 independent stationary gates per scenario, single center,
(a) efficiency .01, gate 20 us and (b) ideal efficiency 1, gate 1 us, MW off. The
second exposes deviations from Poisson rather than hiding single-emitter correlations.
These reference gates do not include a center ensemble or the CW detector background.

before numerical work. Export rejects changed sources/configs/numerical tables,
includes persisted user references, equation maps, attribution archive, environment,
source snapshots and complete SHA256 checksums, and refuses output-directory reuse.

benchmarks have separate labels. Existing historical outputs/checksums are immutable.

runtimes; numerical inputs/arrays and source bytes are supplied.
